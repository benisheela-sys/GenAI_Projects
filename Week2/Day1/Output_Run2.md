## General Guidelines

- Daily water intake: 2.5–3 liters per day.
- Avoid sugary beverages, sweets, and fruit juices.
- Prefer whole fruits over fruit juices.
- Use millets, brown rice, hand-pounded rice, and whole grains frequently.
- Include vegetables in at least two meals daily.
- Eat meals at consistent times to support blood sugar control.
- Aim for 30–45 minutes of walking or light exercise most days of the week.
- Limit fried snacks, bakery items, and packaged foods.
