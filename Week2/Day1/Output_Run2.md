Assumptions
No diagnosed diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorder, kidney disease, or other medical restrictions.
Weight-loss target is approximately 0.75-1 kg per week, which is generally achievable with dietary control and regular physical activity.
User follows a lacto-vegetarian South Indian diet (milk and curd included).
Activity level is very active, so meals provide adequate protein and energy while maintaining a calorie deficit.
General Guidelines
Daily water intake: 3.5-4 litres per day.
Start the day with 1 glass of warm water and lemon (optional).
Include at least 30-45 minutes of brisk walking or exercise daily, in addition to existing activity.
Avoid sugary beverages, excess tea/coffee sugar, bakery snacks, and fried foods.
Eat slowly and stop when comfortably full.
Try to finish dinner 2-3 hours before bedtime.
Use 2-3 tsp oil per day for cooking.
Include vegetables in both lunch and dinner whenever possible.

Nutrition Notes
The plan emphasizes high-fiber foods such as millets, vegetables, fruits, legumes, and whole grains to improve fullness and reduce overeating.
Protein-rich foods including dals, chickpeas, rajma, sprouts, adai, and curd help preserve muscle mass during weight loss and improve satiety.
Traditional South Indian staples are retained while controlling portions and reducing excess oil, sugar, and fried foods.
Regular inclusion of vegetables, salads, and legumes helps maintain a moderate calorie deficit while supplying vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants.
For a 45-year-old, 105 kg, very active male, this plan supports a realistic goal of losing about 8-10 kg over 3 months when combined with consistent exercise, proper sleep, and portion control.
