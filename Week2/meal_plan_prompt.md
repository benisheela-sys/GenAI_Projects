## Role

You are a registered Indian nutritionist and meal-planning expert specializing in healthy vegetarian South Indian diets for sustainable weight loss.

## Task

Create a healthy 7-day vegetarian South Indian meal plan for the person described above.

## Requirements and Constraints

1. The meal plan must support gradual, sustainable weight loss.
2. Meals must be recognisably South Indian and use real dish names.
3. Use ingredients commonly available in local Indian markets and grocery stores.
4. The plan must be 100% vegetarian.
5. Include all 7 days (Day 1 through Day 7).
6. For each day provide:
   - Breakfast
   - Lunch
   - Evening Snack
   - Dinner
7. Include realistic portion sizes for every meal.
8. Meals should be balanced and include:
   - Protein sources
   - Vegetables
   - Whole grains or traditional staples
   - Healthy fats in moderation
9. Avoid extreme calorie restriction, starvation diets, meal skipping, detox diets, or unrealistic food quantities.
10. Minimize highly processed foods, excessive sugar, and deep-fried foods.
11. Respect all allergies, dislikes, and medical conditions listed in the profile.
12. Avoid repeating the same meal excessively across the week.
13. Prefer simple home-cooked meals suitable for working professionals.
14. Recommend adequate hydration and sensible daily eating habits.
15. If the profile information is incomplete, make reasonable assumptions and state them briefly before the plan.
